# Intellectual Property and Licensing

## Open-source scope

Unless a file states otherwise, repository code and documentation are licensed under Apache License 2.0. Contributions are accepted under the same license. Apache License 2.0 permits use, modification, and distribution while preserving notices and its patent terms.

## Commercial scope

The repository license does not grant:

- access to the official managed FACF network;
- provider verification or a place in its registry;
- customer contracts, pricing, service levels, support, or settlement;
- production credentials, operational data, reputation records, or demand relationships;
- rights to Cloud Computing Oy or FACF names and marks beyond legally permitted descriptive use.

Separately distributed enterprise software, hosted services, policies, and commercial agreements may use separate terms. Their boundary must be explicit; the public repository must never imply that proprietary material is Apache-licensed.

## Contributor provenance

Contributors must have the right to submit their work and must not include confidential information, incompatible code, model weights, datasets, or third-party marks. A Developer Certificate of Origin or contributor agreement may be introduced before accepting substantial external contributions.

## Legal review

Trademark registration, contributor terms, provider agreements, customer terms, privacy notices, tax treatment, export controls, and regulated-data commitments require qualified legal review before commercial launch.
