# Control and Defensibility

## Objective

Keep the protocol credible and adoptable while ensuring that Cloud Computing Oy
can build a durable, profitable operating position. Control must come from
valuable service, trusted relationships, operational excellence, and protected
commercial assets—not by misleading contributors about what is open.

## Open Layer

The public repository should contain:

- protocol specifications and schemas;
- community provider agent;
- conformance fixtures and simulator;
- baseline runtime adapters;
- security and trust requirements;
- a development/reference broker sufficient for interoperability;
- SDKs and local development tooling.

Apache-2.0 supports adoption and commercial use. It does not grant trademark
rights.

## Cloud Computing Oy Controlled Layer

- Official managed FACF network and customer contracts.
- Official FACF name, logos, certification marks, and verified-provider badges.
- Production provider registry and identity/reputation history.
- Buyer relationships, demand aggregation, and workload history.
- Managed scheduling policy, forecasting, fraud controls, and operational data.
- Billing, settlement, tax, support, and SLA processes.
- Enterprise administration, private-fabric management, and compliance evidence.
- Proprietary connectors or optimisations that are not required for protocol
  interoperability.

## Defensibility Flywheel

```text
more qualified demand
  -> better provider utilisation
  -> stronger provider participation
  -> broader model/region availability
  -> better reliability and economics
  -> more qualified demand
```

The durable data asset is aggregated operational evidence: offer accuracy,
completion, latency, reconciliation, incidents, model/runtime performance, and
buyer workload patterns. Personal data and prompt bodies are not part of the
moat.

## Governance Controls

- Cloud Computing Oy remains initial maintainer and release authority.
- Protected branches, signed releases, public ADRs, and a contributor licence
  policy protect provenance and release integrity.
- Protocol governance may broaden after commercial and technical maturity, but
  the official managed network remains a separate service.
- Conformance criteria are public; certification decisions and marks are
  controlled and revocable.

## Trademark Strategy

Before broad promotion, evaluate registration of `FACF` and `Federated AI
Compute Fabric` in relevant jurisdictions and classes. Until legal review is
complete, documentation must not claim registered rights. The project should
publish a trademark policy before third parties use official compatibility or
provider badges.

## Fork Risk

An open protocol can be forked. FACF should not try to prevent lawful forks.
Instead, make the official network preferable through verified supply, demand,
service quality, integrations, reputation history, customer support, and trusted
branding. Avoid dependencies that make the open implementation intentionally
useless; that would weaken adoption and contributor trust.

## Preventing Loss of Control

- Do not put production credentials, provider identities, pricing agreements,
  fraud signals, customer policies, or private operations into the public repo.
- Separate open repositories from commercial deployment repositories.
- Require explicit approval before protocol trademarks or official badges are
  used commercially.
- Keep customer and provider contracts directly with Cloud Computing Oy.
- Build demand before opening unrestricted supply enrollment.
- Retain human approval for trust-level promotion, disputes, and sensitive
  routing until evidence supports automation.

## Licensing Decisions Still Required

- Whether external contributors sign a Developer Certificate of Origin or a
  Contributor Licence Agreement.
- Which enterprise components remain proprietary or use a separate licence.
- Patent and defensive-publication strategy for novel scheduling or verification.

Owner: Cloud Computing Oy leadership with qualified legal counsel. Gate: before
accepting substantial external contributions or launching paid service.
