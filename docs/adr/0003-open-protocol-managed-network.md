# ADR 0003: Open Protocol and Commercial Managed Network

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-08-26

## Context

Adoption benefits from inspectable protocols and low-friction provider software. Sustainable operation requires trusted enrollment, policy, demand aggregation, support, billing, and accountable service delivery.

## Decision

FACF publishes the protocol, documentation, and reference components under an open-source license. Cloud Computing Oy retains control of the official managed network, official branding, verified-provider registry, commercial services, and proprietary enterprise operations where separately identified.

## Consequences

Independent compatible networks are possible. They do not automatically inherit official status, participants, reputation, contracts, or trademarks. The company must win commercially through service quality and network value rather than artificial protocol incompatibility.
