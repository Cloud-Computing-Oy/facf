- [NATS JetStream documentation](https://docs.nats.io/nats-concepts/jetstream) — durable event and work-distribution concepts.
- [OpenTelemetry documentation](https://opentelemetry.io/docs/) — vendor-neutral telemetry conventions.

- [Gensyn documentation](https://docs.gensyn.ai/) — an example of decentralized machine-learning network research and implementation.
