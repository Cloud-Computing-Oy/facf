# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, professional, and harassment-free project for participants of every background and level of experience.

## Expected behavior

- discuss ideas and evidence without attacking people;
- welcome questions and constructive disagreement;
- respect privacy, consent, attribution, and legal obligations;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, and deliberate disruption;
- disclose security issues privately and conflicts of interest transparently.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation, or ban participants whose conduct is harmful to the project or community. Reports should be made privately to a verified Cloud Computing Oy representative through the organization's official channels. Maintainers will limit disclosure to what is reasonably necessary to review and respond.

## Scope

This policy applies in project repositories, communication channels, events, and public interactions where a person represents the FACF project.
